# ADR 0004: License

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-05

## Context

Mimir Lite is published as a public tool. Users and contributors need to know under which terms they may use, change, and redistribute it, and who holds the copyright.

## Decision

License the repository under the MIT License. The license text is [`LICENSE.md`](../../LICENSE.md), with the copyright line "The Mimir Lite authors"; the authors are listed in [`AUTHORS.md`](../../AUTHORS.md). The files use the `.md` extension so the repository's allowlist `.gitignore` tracks them. The license text is legal text and stays English-only; it has no `.es.md` translation.

## Consequences

- Anyone may use, modify, and redistribute Mimir Lite under the MIT terms.
- Contributions are accepted under the same license.
- New authors are added to `AUTHORS.md`; the copyright line does not change.
